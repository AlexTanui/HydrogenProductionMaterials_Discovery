Energy bias diagnosis

Ethanol CCSD(T) · Gold train/validation · October 1, 2026

Main finding

The selected 200-epoch model overpredicts all evaluated training and validation energies. Validation mean error is +1.815 kcal/mol. The residual distribution is narrower than at epoch 100, but its positive offset is larger.

No correction or retraining was applied. No test predictions were used in this analysis.

What this means

Squared mean bias accounts for 93.2% of validation MSE at epoch 200. Training and validation show a similar positive shift. These aggregate energy metrics do not show a large train-validation gap, but they do not rule out overfitting generally.

A constant energy offset does not affect the derivative used to calculate forces. This helps explain why force accuracy and absolute-energy accuracy can move in different directions; the optimization cause remains unconfirmed.
